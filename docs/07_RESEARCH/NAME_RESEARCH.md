# Naming Research — Preliminary October 2026

**Status:** Implementation planning | **Date:** 2026-10-08 | **Working title:** Universal Converter

## Important limitation
This is **search-based screening**, not confirmed availability, trademark clearance, ownership lookup or guaranteed Mac App Store title reservation. Search engines may miss App Store results and private business uses. Before selecting, search App Store in multiple regions, Apple developer App Store Connect naming, USPTO/WIPO trademarks, domains and GitHub; check phonetic similarity.

## Further-candidate shortlist — **unverified**, not declared available
- **PixelFerry** — personable, image-oriented; initial broad search did not establish a known exact app collision.
- **FluxCrate** — abstract, extensible beyond media; initial broad search did not establish a known exact app collision.
- **ConvertHarbor** — straightforward but close to existing FormatHarbor; substantial similarity risk.

## Criteria
Short and easy to pronounce; distinctive spelling; clear Mac utility personality; good icon potential; no established adjacent converter; usable for future image/audio/document conversion; align with Jewhurst Engineering without forcing its name into the product. A name with no visible web result is *not* proved unused.
